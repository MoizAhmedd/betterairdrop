Description

A menu-bar app that renames photos you AirDrop to your Mac by date, city and content:
IMG_4821.HEIC → 2026-09-21_toronto_walnut-lamp-on-oak-sideboard.jpg

- HEIC to JPEG
- Undo any rename
- Names on your Mac (Apple Vision, Apple Intelligence) or with Claude
- Open source (MIT)
