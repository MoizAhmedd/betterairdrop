Terms and refunds

The app is free and open source (MIT). Pro is a paid licence key that unlocks extra features.

The licence

  • One key works on up to 3 Macs you use. Deactivate a Mac to free its slot.
  • One payment, no subscription. Updates are included for as long as BetterAirdrop is made.
  • Don't share or resell your key.

Refunds

Within 14 days of buying, for any reason. Email hello@betterairdrop.com from the address you bought with. The key stops working once refunded.

Payments

Payments go through Polar, the merchant of record. Polar takes payment, charges any sales tax, and sends the receipt and your key.

The fine print

BetterAirdrop is provided as is, without warranty. It renames and moves files; Undo puts them back, but keep backups of anything that matters.
